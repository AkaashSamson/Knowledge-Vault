# Knowledge Vault Index

Welcome to the Personal Knowledge Vault. This repository represents an AI-native, interconnected knowledge corpus built to conform with Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2.

## Core Directories

- [00-Inbox](/00-Inbox): Staging area for raw materials, clippings, and items awaiting triage.
- [01-Sources](/01-Sources/records): Master catalog of original materials and their corresponding metadata records.
- [02-Wiki](/02-Wiki): Permanent, reusable, and interconnected knowledge concepts, topics, entities, and research questions.
- [03-Outputs](/03-Outputs): Generated deliverables including articles, presentations, reports, and scripts.
- [04-Meta](/04-Meta/index.md): Administrative index, vault constitution, taxonomy, templates, logs, and agent skills.
- [05-Bundles](/05-Bundles): External, modular OKF knowledge bundles imported from third-party sources.

## Getting Started

- Consult the [Vault Constitution](/04-Meta/constitution.md) for architectural principles, workflows, and governance rules.
- Review the [Taxonomy Reference](/04-Meta/taxonomy.md) before applying or proposing tags.
- Review the [Source Index](/04-Meta/source-index.md) for catalogued sources.
- Review the [Changelog](/04-Meta/logs/index.md) for update history.
